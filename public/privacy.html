Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Short version: we collect as little as possible. Strangers only see your nickname. We never get your real name, email or photo from Google, Facebook or Discord. Camera video goes directly between you and your opponent and never passes through our servers. We don't sell your data.

1. What we collect

  • Sign-in account ID. When you sign in with Google, Facebook or Discord, we receive a unique ID for your account with that service. We ask only for the minimum: Google's openid scope, Facebook's public_profile (from which we read only your ID), and Discord's identify scope (from which we read only your ID).
  • Your nickname. The name you choose to show other players.
  • Game data. Rating, rank, peak rating, wins, losses, draws, streaks, answer accuracy, the IDs of questions you've seen recently (so you don't get repeats), and the players you've blocked.
  • Reports. If you report a player, or someone reports you, we store the report: the reason, both players' IDs, the reported player's nickname, and the last 20 chat messages of that match.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may log IP addresses and request information to run and secure the service.

2. What we don't collect

  • Your real name, email address, profile photo, friends list or contacts from any sign-in provider.
  • Recordings of camera video or audio. We never see or store them.
  • Chat history in general. Messages are passed between you and your opponent in real time and are not saved, except the last few messages kept in memory during a match so they can be attached if someone files a report.
  • Payment information. (If we add paid features later, a payment processor such as Stripe will handle card details, not us.)

3. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To match you with other players near your rating, run matches, and keep the leaderboard.
  • To pick questions at the right difficulty and avoid repeats.
  • To keep Trivegle safe: enforcing the rules, reviewing reports, and banning accounts that break them.
  • To fix bugs and protect the service from abuse.

4. Camera mode

Camera mode is optional and for players 18 and older. When you use it:

  • Video and audio are sent directly between your browser and your opponent's browser (peer-to-peer using WebRTC). Our server only helps the two browsers find each other.
  • Because the connection is direct, your opponent's browser can see your IP address, which can reveal your approximate location (city or region). If that concerns you, use Text mode. If we add a relay (TURN) server, some connections are routed through it instead; the relay forwards encrypted media and does not store it.
  • We cannot see, record or moderate video in real time. Please report anyone who breaks the rules. Your opponent could record their own screen without our knowledge, so don't show anything you wouldn't want shared.

5. What other players see

Your nickname, rating and rank are shown to opponents and on the public leaderboard, along with your wins, losses, draws, best streak and accuracy. Anything you type in chat, or show on camera, is seen by your opponent.

6. Who we share information with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that run Trivegle for us: our hosting provider (such as Render or Railway) and our database provider (such as Neon), which store our data.
  • Sign-in providers (Google, Facebook, Discord) as part of signing in. Their own privacy policies apply to your accounts with them.
  • Advertising partners, if ads are turned on (see below).
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

7. Cookies and local storage

  • Session cookie (trivegle_session): keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Required for the site to work.
  • Sign-in cookie (trivegle_oauth): a short-lived security check used during sign-in, deleted after 10 minutes or once sign-in finishes.
  • Local storage in your browser: remembers your preferences, such as Text or Camera mode, and the age and rules checkboxes. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

8. Advertising

Trivegle may show ads from Google AdSense on the home page, the matchmaking screen and the results screen, never during questions. When ads are on, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required (for example in the EU and UK), we'll ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

9. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: up to 12 months, then deleted automatically, so we can spot repeat offenders.
  • Hosting logs: according to our hosting provider's retention settings.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all your game data at any time. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. You'll disappear from the leaderboard, and signing in again will create a brand-new account.

  1. Sign in to Trivegle.
  2. Click Delete account below (also available from Your stats on the home page).
  3. Confirm.

This also covers data-deletion requests for Facebook Login: deleting your Trivegle account removes everything linked to your Facebook ID. Reports about your behavior may be kept for up to 12 months for safety reasons, as described above. You can also email us at the address below.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to some uses of it. Use the delete button above, or contact us for anything else. We'll respond within 30 days.

12. Children

Trivegle is not for children under 13, and Camera mode is only for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Trivegle, contact us or report them in a match, and we'll delete the account.

13. Security

Sign-in sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies sent over HTTPS. We store only what's listed above. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll let you know on the site before they take effect.
