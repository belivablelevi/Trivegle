    Rules & safety

    • You must be 13 or older to play. Camera mode is 18+ only.
    • Camera mode: keep it clothed and clean. No nudity, no showing others without consent, no recording people.
    • No harassment, hate speech, sexual content, or threats.
    • Don't share personal info (address, phone, socials). Links and contact handles are removed automatically.
    • Use Report if someone breaks the rules — reports include the chat transcript and are reviewed.
    • Use Block to never be matched with someone again.
    • Bots are always labelled as bots.
